# Brag Plan: Trip to Japan

## What is this app?
A Japan trip planner: pick from 152 places across 14 regions, set your dates, and it builds a route-aware, day-by-day itinerary with a map, a timed day plan and a list of what to book ahead.

## The angle
A calm, confident product demo. No mock-ups: every UI shot is the app's real React components, rendered by the running app with real catalog data and captured as live DOM (not screenshots), then driven by a cursor. The pitch is the flow itself — three steps from "where do I even start" to a finished plan.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The app's own full-screen hero — Mt. Fuji, "Discover Japan" — and a cursor pressing "Start Your Journey".

## Key moments (the middle)
- Cursor picks three places in the photo catalog; the counter climbs 7 → 10 and the Tokyo badge reaches "5 picked".
- Dates step: the fit check pops in — "Your 10 places fit comfortably" — then "Build itinerary".
- The itinerary: real map with numbered day pins, then a click on the "Day 1" chip jumps to the day card and its timed "Suggested day plan".

## Outro / punchline
"Trip to Japan" with the app's red-sun mark, and the site's own line: "Plan Your Trip".

## User flow worth showing
Choose places → pick dates (fit check) → built itinerary (map → day plan).

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: quiet, warm travel-product film; the real UI does the talking
- Interpretation: clean slides and soft crossfades, short captions in the app's own words, motion snapped to the beat but never rushed.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse interface accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (steady, clean), volume 0.32, fade out over the outro
- Music cue guidance: preset read (109.96 BPM). Strong cues used: 8.74s (cut to dates), 13.11s (map reveal), 18.56s (logo). Beat grid for clicks: 4.91, 6.00, 7.09 (every other beat).
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (extraction helper not installed; documented, not blocking)
- SFX posture: sparse; one soft click per cursor press, one soft impact on the logo
- Restraint rule: no sound without a visible action
