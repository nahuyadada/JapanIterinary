Trip to Japan
From a wish list to a day-by-day plan
152 places · 14 regions · map, day plan and booking checklist
Planning a trip to Japan usually means a dozen browser tabs, a spreadsheet,
and a lot of guessing. Trip to Japan turns that into three simple steps.
It all starts on one page. Press Start Your Journey, and you are in the planner.
Step one: choose your places.
The catalog has one hundred fifty-two spots across fourteen regions,
each with a photo and a short description.
Search, filter by category, and tap the ones you want.
Your picks are always one click away.
Step two: pick your dates. The planner tells you right away whether your places fit comfortably,
or whether some days would be overbooked.
Already holding a ticket for a certain day? Lock that place to its date.
Step three: your itinerary. Every stop appears on the map, numbered by day.
A checklist shows what to book before you fly.
Each city comes with a recommended area to stay, and each day gets a suggested schedule,
with travel times and directions. Want a change? Move any place to another day.
When you are happy with it, share the trip.
The link opens a step-by-step guide you can follow on your phone.
